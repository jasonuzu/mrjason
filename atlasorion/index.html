Who we are

Schools got the technology. Nobody built the bridge to Monday morning.

Atlas Orion was started by teachers and engineers who kept watching the same thing happen: a school buys good software, and six weeks later nobody opens it. Not because it was bad — because it never fitted the actual shape of a teaching week.

So we build for that shape. Our tools open fast, work on the device already in the room, and ask for nothing a teacher wouldn't have written down anyway. They don't replace your judgement. They give it better material to work with.
